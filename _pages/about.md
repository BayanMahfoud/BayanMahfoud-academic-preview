## About Me

I am an undergraduate Artificial Intelligence student and independent researcher interested in the intersection of artificial intelligence, computational neuroscience, cognitive architectures, spiking neural networks, and consciousness science.

My academic interests focus on understanding how intelligent systems can develop organized internal processes and more flexible forms of cognition.

I am currently building the theoretical and technical foundations for an independent research project involving computational modeling and spiking neural networks. The project is currently in its literature-study and theory-development stages, and specific technical details will be shared gradually as the work progresses.

## Research Interests

- Artificial Intelligence
- Computational Neuroscience
- Spiking Neural Networks
- Cognitive Architectures
- Consciousness Science
- Self-Modeling and Agency
- Metacognition
- Reproducible AI Research

## Research Approach

My research approach is based on studying scientific literature, developing clear theoretical definitions, translating concepts into computational models, and evaluating proposed mechanisms through controlled and reproducible experiments.

I am particularly interested in research that examines internal organization rather than relying only on externally observable behavior.
