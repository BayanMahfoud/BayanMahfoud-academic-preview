## About

I am a fourth-year undergraduate Artificial Intelligence student from Syria and the founder and lead researcher of [MIND-B](https://mindb-qk5njhtw.manus.space/ ), a personal research laboratory I am building from the ground up.

My research interests are located at the intersection of artificial intelligence, NeuroAI, consciousness, and computational neuroscience. My long-term interest is the study of consciousness and disorders of consciousness, with particular attention to computational modelling, neural dynamics, and neural data analysis.

I am currently at the literature-study, theory-development, and foundation-building stage. I am strengthening my background in neuroscience, mathematical modelling, scientific Python, machine learning, neural signal processing, and reproducible research.

My current priority is not to present a premature solution. It is to understand how brain states are studied, how disorders of consciousness are assessed, what computational and neural interventions have been explored, and which scientific questions remain open.

I document my learning process, literature study, technical experiments, questions, limitations, and future work. MIND-B provides the laboratory structure for this path, while this website represents the researcher behind it.

## MIND-B Research Laboratory

[MIND-B](https://mindb-qk5njhtw.manus.space/ ) is a personal research laboratory led by one researcher. It documents a real path from learning and implementation to early computational experiments and, eventually, original research projects.

The laboratory is currently in its foundation stage. Its work is organized around research seasons, with each season focusing on a defined stage of learning, experimentation, review, or project development.

The laboratory website publishes major milestones, seasons, projects, and releases. Technical code, computational experiments, and reproducible files are shared through GitHub when they are ready.

## Research status

This work is currently exploratory and foundational. I am not presenting a clinical treatment, a validated neural intervention, or a completed research result.

The immediate goal is to understand the field deeply enough to formulate a precise, testable, and scientifically responsible research hypothesis.
