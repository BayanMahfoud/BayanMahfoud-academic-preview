## Research Direction

My current research direction is being developed through careful literature study and theory development. I am not presenting a completed research result, clinical solution, or validated neural intervention.

I am studying questions related to consciousness and disorders of consciousness, including how researchers characterize brain states, how neural dynamics are measured, and how computational models may help formulate testable explanations.

The specific project direction is still being refined. The next stage will be defined by the concepts, methods, and research questions that remain most scientifically tractable after the foundation-building phase.

## Research approach

My approach begins with careful reading of scientific literature and the development of clear theoretical definitions. I then aim to translate well-defined concepts into computational models, measurable predictions, controlled comparisons, and reproducible experiments.

At the current stage, the priority is understanding the problem before proposing a mechanism. I distinguish between established evidence, working hypotheses, computational observations, and clinical claims.

Potential neural interventions are treated as safety-critical research questions. No intervention mechanism is currently presented as a solution or as a clinical claim.
